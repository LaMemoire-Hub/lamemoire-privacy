Privacy Policy

La Mémoire (LaMemoireIDN) · Last updated: September 2026

Who we are

La Mémoire is a digital planner shop on Etsy (etsy.com/shop/LaMemoireIDN). We create hyperlinked PDF planners and journals for e-ink tablets including reMarkable and Kindle Scribe.

What we collect

Our Pinterest integration accesses only our own Pinterest account to publish product pin images to our own boards. We do not collect, store, or process any data from Pinterest users, visitors, or third parties.

How we use Pinterest

We use the Pinterest API solely to post product images and links from our own Etsy shop to our own Pinterest boards. No user data is read, stored, or shared. No analytics or tracking data is collected from Pinterest users.

Data storage

We store only our own API access token locally on our own device for authentication purposes. No other data from Pinterest is stored.

Third-party sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. Our Pinterest integration is used exclusively for posting our own product content.

Contact
